05 Rules

Phone / doomscrolling reset

  • No social media immediately after waking
  • No phone during study sessions
  • Log out of the most distracting apps for 14 days
  • Disable unnecessary notifications
  • Keep the phone away from your bed
  • Use a fixed entertainment window instead of opening apps whenever bored
Emergency rule
Lock phone → stand up → drink water → start a 5-minute task. Don't negotiate for another 30 minutes.

Urge management

Use a practical, non-shame-based approach. Identify your actual triggers — being alone with unrestricted phone access, boredom, stress, staying in bed while awake, late-night scrolling — and address those specifically.

Protocol
Recognize the urge without judgment → put the phone away, change environment → wait 10 minutes doing something else (walk, stretch, wash face) → return to your planned activity.

If you slip, record the trigger and adjust — don't restart your whole life from zero. If this feels uncontrollable or causes real distress, talk to a qualified mental-health professional.
